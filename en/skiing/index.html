Skiing in Megève & Combloux

Two passes, two sides of the valley

There are two lift passes: Évasion Mont-Blanc, which covers everything, and the cheaper Portes du Mont-Blanc, limited to Combloux, Le Jaillet and La Giettaz.

On the slopes, Megève has two sides that are not linked on skis:
  • Rochebrune, Côte 2000, Mont d'Arbois, the Princesse, Saint-Gervais and Saint-Nicolas-de-Véroce are linked together (Évasion pass).
  • Le Jaillet, Combloux and La Giettaz are on the other side, linked together but not to Mont d'Arbois or Rochebrune (Portes du Mont-Blanc or Évasion pass).
To switch sides, cross Megève by car or on the free Meg'bus. Les Contamines, also included in Évasion, is separate, by car.

🏔️ Rochebrune & Côte 2000

Évasion

The "historic" area right above the village: the Rochebrune cable car from the centre, runs through the forest and, at Côte 2000, the highest point of the sector. The Rocharbois cable car links it to Mont d'Arbois.

🌲 Mont d'Arbois · Princesse → Saint-Gervais

Évasion

The largest sector: the Mont d'Arbois and Princesse (Demi-Quartier) gondolas, then the link to Saint-Gervais and Saint-Nicolas-de-Véroce, up to Mont Joly (2,350 m) facing Mont Blanc.

☀️ Le Jaillet → Combloux & La Giettaz

Portes du Mont-Blanc + Évasion

The sunny, quieter side, ideal for families. From the Jaillet gondola you reach Combloux, the Tête du Torraz and La Giettaz (Aravis side). The first morning runs, facing Mont Blanc, are often empty.

🏡 Combloux

Portes du Mont-Blanc + Évasion

"The pearl of Mont Blanc": a family area among fir trees and pastures, with free car parks at the foot of the slopes (Brons, la Cry, Cuchet), a beginners' area and an adaptive-ski area.

⛰️ Les Contamines-Hauteluce

Évasion

Included in Évasion but about forty minutes away by road: a higher area, handy when snow is short lower down.

💡 Tips & discounts

  • Under 5: free pass (only the €2 hands-free card is charged). 80 and over: 50% off the adult price.
  • Megève Skideal: buy online at least 3 days ahead and the Évasion day pass starts at €32 (dynamic pricing).
  • 6 days for the price of 5 on Évasion when bought online 10 days ahead (€289.50 adult instead of €324.50).
  • Family: 10% off for at least 4 passes bought together, including 2 children (1 to 21 days).
  • Évasion season pass on offer until 30 November 2026: €890 adult instead of €1,189. Same deadline for the "7 Liberty days" (7 non-consecutive days, winter and summer) at €387.
  • Included with an Évasion pass bought in Megève: the free Meg'bus, the Luge Princesse (3 km snow sledging run) and 50% off the Palais Balnéoforme spa (2 to 21-day passes).
  • Rescue insurance (optional): about €3.50 a day. Worth it: a rescue on the slopes is expensive.
  • Adaptive skiing at Portes du Mont-Blanc: 50% off for the skier with a disability and their companion (at the ticket office).

🐣 Beginners & pedestrians

  • Rochebrune / Mont d'Arbois beginner pass: €29 a day (carpets and small drag lifts, Chamois gondola, Rocharbois cable car). Petit Rochebrune only: €15, at the ticket office.
  • Portes du Mont-Blanc beginner pass: €18 a day (Mowgli, Brons, Garettes, Plan, Covagnet, Grande Rare, Pattes carpet), at the ticket office.
  • Pedestrians: €23 for an Évasion day (€20.50 child) to ride up to mountain restaurants; on Portes du Mont-Blanc, €11.50 one way and €19.50 return (child €9.50 / €15).

✨ Also

🎿 Cross-country skiing

About forty kilometres in Megève, on three sites: la Livraz – Côte 2000 (the main one, towards the altiport), the Mont d'Arbois golf course and Odier, linked to Combloux. A trail pass is required, sold at the welcome chalet.

🥾 Ski touring

12 signposted routes on the Portes du Mont-Blanc area, keeping tourers apart from skiers. The "Combloux Rando" pass lets you ride the Beauregard, Pertuis and Jouty chairlifts.

🛷 Sledging

The Luge Princesse, 3 km on snow from the Princesse, free with an Évasion pass bought in Megève, and the Luge 4S in Combloux.

👶 Ski lessons

Ski schools (ESF and independent schools) in both Megève and Combloux, with snow gardens for little ones. Book early for Christmas and February.

Good to know

Which lift pass do I need to ski in Megève?

For Rochebrune, Côte 2000, Mont d'Arbois and Saint-Gervais you need the Évasion Mont-Blanc pass. If you only ski the Jaillet, Combloux and La Giettaz side, the Portes du Mont-Blanc pass is enough and cheaper (€52.50 an adult day vs €65).

Can you ski from Megève to Combloux?

Yes, via Le Jaillet: from the Jaillet gondola in Megève, the runs lead to Combloux and La Giettaz. However, Le Jaillet is not linked on skis to Rochebrune or Mont d'Arbois: you cross the village (free Meg'bus).

Where can I park for free to ski?

In Megève: the Princesse (Demi-Quartier) and Côte 2000 car parks. In Combloux: Les Brons, la Cry and Cuchet, all free at the foot of the slopes. At Le Jaillet, parking is a blue zone (disc).

When do the slopes open this winter?

Megève plans an early opening on the weekend of 5-6 December 2026, partial opening from 12 December and full opening on 19 December, until 4 April 2027. Portes du Mont-Blanc is open from 19 December 2026 to 21 March 2027. Everything depends on snow.

How can I pay less for my lift pass?

Buy online in advance (Évasion day from €32 three days ahead, 6 days for the price of 5 ten days ahead), ski early or late season (20% off), use the family discount, and keep your hands-free card to reload it.

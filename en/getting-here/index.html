Getting here & around

Car, train, bus or plane: how to get to Megève, then park and get around the villages hassle-free.

🚗 By car

  • From Geneva: about 70 km, around 1 hour without traffic, via the A40 motorway (Sallanches exit) then the road up to Megève.
  • In winter, equipment is compulsory from 1 November to 31 March in Haute-Savoie (Mountain Law): winter tyres, or snow chains / snow socks in the boot.
  • On holiday Saturdays, leave early: the roads up to the resorts get very busy from late morning.

🚆 By train

  • The nearest station is Sallanches-Combloux-Megève, served by TER regional trains.
  • From the station, regional bus Y83 goes up to Combloux, Megève and Praz-sur-Arly (about 25 minutes to Megève).

✈️ By plane

  • The nearest airport is Geneva (about 1 hour by car). Lyon and Annecy are also options.
  • Coaches (FlixBus) link Geneva and Lyon to Sallanches bus station, then take bus Y83 to Megève.
  • Several companies offer shared or private airport transfers: book ahead in high season.

🚌 Getting around without a car

  • Meg'bus: Megève's free shuttle links the village to the lifts (lines 1 to 3 in winter, 1 and 2 in summer).
  • Regional lines Y82 (Chamonix · Saint-Gervais · Combloux · Megève · Praz-sur-Arly) and Y83 (Sallanches · Combloux · Megève · Praz-sur-Arly) link the villages.
  • Combloux and Praz-sur-Arly also run winter shuttles to the slopes: timetables at the tourist offices.
  • Montenvélo self-service e-bikes in several Pays du Mont-Blanc villages, including Combloux.

🅿️ Parking in Megève

  • Covered car parks: Village (route Nationale), Casino (by the pedestrian area, 200 spaces, 1.90 m height limit), Tourist Office (rue de la Poste), Rochebrune and Mont d'Arbois (297 spaces at the gondola, 2.10 m).
  • Open-air Palais car park: 389 spaces, a short walk from the centre.
  • The first 30 minutes are free in most car parks. Information: Megève Parkings, +33 4 50 21 18 30.
  • In Combloux, La Cry car park (100 spaces) is at the foot of the ski area.

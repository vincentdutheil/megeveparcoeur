📲 The app

Megève par cœur in your pocket

Events, snow, webcams and your favourite outings on your home screen. Free, no ads, no App Store needed.

Install the app

✅ The app is installed on this device. Welcome!

👋 You're in Instagram's browser (or another app's). It can't install Megève par cœur: first open the site in your real browser.

Tap the three dots ⋯ at the top right, then “Open in external browser” (or “Open in Safari”).

Tap the button below, or the three dots ⋮ at the top right, then “Open in Chrome”.

🌐 Open in Chrome

🍏 iPhone / iPad

  1. Open megeveparcoeur.fr in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button ⬆︎ at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Choose “Add to Home Screen”, then Add.
  4. Open the app from its new ❤ icon to turn on notifications.

🤖 Android

  1. Open megeveparcoeur.fr in Chrome.
  2. Tap the Install button below, or the ⋮ menu then “Install app”.
  3. The app appears on your home screen like any other.

What the app does

⭐My picks

Tap ☆ on any event to save it to “My picks”.

📶Works offline

The last pages you viewed stay available in the mountains, even without signal.

⚡Shortcuts

Long-press the icon: events, this weekend, snow, webcams, kids' corner.

🔒Privacy first

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your favourites stay on your phone.

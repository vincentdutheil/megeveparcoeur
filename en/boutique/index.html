The partnership

One shop, two passions for the mountains

Megève par cœur and the Coupe des Banques Genevoises have teamed up to create a joint charity shop. The shop is hosted on the Coupe's website: that's where the products are and where purchases are made.

Good to know

Your questions

How much of my purchase goes to the charity?

100% of the profits. Once the products and their making are paid for, everything left goes to a charity.

Which charity will be supported?

It is being chosen and will be announced very soon, here and on Instagram @megeveparcoeur.

Where can I buy the products?

On the Coupe des Banques Genevoises online shop, which handles orders and shipping. The "Go to the shop" button takes you there.

Want to support the cause?

Head to the Coupe des Banques Genevoises charity shop to discover the products as soon as they are released.

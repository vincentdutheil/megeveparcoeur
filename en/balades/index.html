⛑️ Before you go

  • Trails inside the ski area are only open during lift hours, and never at night.
  • Stay on the marked routes (purple markers for snowshoes), and do not walk in the cross-country ski tracks.
  • Check the snow & weather and the webcams, and ask the tourist office which trails are open.
  • Snowshoe hire at the village sports shops; guided outings with the mountain guide offices.

Times and elevation gains as given by the tourist offices, for guidance only. Sources: Megève Tourisme, Combloux tourist office, Praz-sur-Arly tourist office, Haute-Savoie Tourisme.
